# Busting

When somebody draws a card that busts them, make the card hover over their hand, and then slam down with a intense shake and then have them bust. it must feel impactful

# Second life

When the card saves, then show: make the card which would bust in the center of the screen big, and then the second chance flies up to it, and rips the card. Then the second chance card flies to the top like an angel.
