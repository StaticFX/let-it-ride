# Circlejerk (instant action)

Give your 2 neighbors (or 1 if its a 2 player game) one of your cards (you can decide)

# Reverse Circlejerk (instant action)

Get from your 2 neightbors (or 1 if its a 2 player game) a card. They decide which one

# Redacted (passive card)

Only you can see your cards when holding this card
