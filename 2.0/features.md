# 2.0

Let it ride 2.0 introduces a brand new game mode: Rolling Rules

This new game mode aims to make let it ride rounds more intense, uniquie and strategic. 

The idea is that users have a second, hidden hand. This hand allows for cards to be played, based on their type they can be played on different times. 

## General Rules

- This game mode builds on top of let-it-ride, the general game rules stay the same. 
- A players second hand is their protected good. Other players cant attack it directly using the "standard" cards from LIR. From now on these cards on the second hand are called, Gambler-Cards. 

- Your points are your currency. The current points are always displayed ingame, somewhere nicely readable. Its the goal to have the most points still, but you can go along, spend them and make even more.

- The second hand persists accross rounds, and does not get reset.
- This gamemode always underlays the extreme house rule.
- A player can hold a max of 5 gambler cards  

The stack rule:

Cards work like a stack, so counters can counter counters.

## Gambler Cards

### Rarities

There are 3 Rarities of cards:

- Common 60%
- Rare 30%
- Jackpot 10%

### Playability

- On turn: Players can only play this card when its their turn
- On other turn: Players can only play this card when its not their turn
- Always: Players can always play this card
- On out: Players can only play this card when out or busted.
- In response: In response to another gambler card
- Passive: Just sits there and has an effect

### Uses

All card are single use

### How players can receive Gambler-Cards

- There are multiple ways to receive gambler cards

1. Drawn randomly from the pool. Whenever they draw a gambler card, they still need to draw a second card, until they draw a normal card.
2. Buy them inbetween rounds in a new shop like system

### Buying Cards

1. With the points a player has gathered they can buy card after a round is done. In there they have a 2 minute time window to buy whatever cards the shop offers. 

There is also an auction house, where one jackpot card is always for auction, and players can bid their points for it.

# Gambler Cards

## Redirect
Rarity: Common
When: On Play
What: When a player draws a card, they can decide if they want to draw it themselfes, or redirect it to another player

## Nullify
Rarity: Common
When: In Response
What: Nullify any actions against you, for example redirect.

## Draw 2
Rarity: Common
When: Always
What: Draw 2 cards in your next turn

## Second Opinion
Rarity: Common
When: Always
What: On your next turn, discard the first card drawn if you dont like it, keep the second one

## Cheating
Rarity: Common
When: Always
What: Pay 100 Pts, on your next turn draw a gamblers cards.

## Fuck it
Rarity: Common
When: On out
What: Resets your points to 0

##  Shuffle
Rarity: Common
When: Always
What: shuffle the deck

## Back to the shop
Rarity: Common
When: Always
What: Sell your gambler cards to the dealer for 70% of their original price

## Deflect
Rarity: Rare
When: On other turn
What: When somebody wants to use a card on your, redirect it to the player

## Nahhh
Rarity: Common
When: Always
What: When another player wants to play a gamblers card, cancell the action. The player keeps the card

## Pouch
Rarity: Rare
When: Passive
What: Have 2 more slots for your gambler cards

## Double down
Rarity: Rare
When: Always
What: Multiply all points you receive or lose this round by 2

## TAXES
Rarity: Rare
When: On turn
What: When another player gains points, receive 5% of them

## Already down
Rarity: Rare
When: On turn
What: The closer you are to 0 points, gain a boost for the rest of the game bonus = clamp(0, 0.10, 0.10 * (1 - current_points / leader_points))

## Loan
Rarity: Rare
When: On turn
What: Lend 200 points from the dealer, pay back 300 points next round

## Foreseer
Rarity: Rare
When: Always
What: Look at the next 3 cards in the dekc

## Revive
Rarity: Rare
When: On out
What: rejoin the game, but lose all your cards

## Not this time
Rarity: Jackpot
When: Always
What: Remove the flip-7 ability this round

## The cooler revive
Rarity: Jackpot
When: On out
What: rejoin the game, but keep your current hand, even if it has duplicate cards

## Trade in
Rarity: Jackpot
When: On turn
What: Trade in all your gambler cards for new ones


## Split the Pot
Rarity: Rare
When: On turn
What: Bank half your current round points immediately. They're safe from busting. You continue the round with the other half at risk.

## Copycat
Rarity: Rare
When: In Response
What: Copy the played gambler card and use it instantly

## Rigged Bid
Rarity: Rare
When: On other turn
What: In the next Auction, Bid over the winning bid by 10% after the auction closed

## Stacked Deck
Rarity: Jackpot
When: On turn
What: Look at the next 5 cards and reorder them

## House Rules
Rarity: Jackpot
When: On turn
What: For the rest of the round, pick one: duplicates don't bust but nobody may stay; or everyone must stay after 4 cards; or all point values are inverted.
