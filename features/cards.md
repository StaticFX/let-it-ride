# Double or Nothing

Type: Instant
Effect: The user has a 50/50 chance to double his points, (this card then converts into a passive with this bonus) or gets bust for this round
Visual: Coin falls from the top, with a win or lose side and then goes around and lands on a side. 

# Slots

Type: Instant (only on the one who drawn it)
Effect: User gets a random card from a slot machine
Visual: A slot machine pops up, then a random card is drawn from it (or none)

# All in

Type: Lobby effect
Effect: All players must bet a card (hidden). The player with the highest card and losest card loses 50% of their points.
Visual: Every player selects a card from their hand.
